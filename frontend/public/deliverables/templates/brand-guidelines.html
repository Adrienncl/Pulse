Usage Guidelines

Primary is used for key actions, headings, and primary UI elements. Secondary supports backgrounds, illustrations, and secondary components. Accent highlights calls-to-action, links, and interactive states. Use Dark as the foundation for text and high-contrast surfaces. Maintain a minimum 4.5:1 contrast ratio for accessibility (WCAG AA).

Brand Voice

Our voice is how {{BRAND_NAME}} speaks to the world. It should feel consistent across every channel and touchpoint.

"{{TAGLINE}}"

Every piece of communication from {{BRAND_NAME}} should reflect our commitment to authenticity, clarity, and purpose. We speak with confidence but never arrogance. We inform without condescending. We inspire without exaggerating.

Our voice is built on four pillars that guide every headline, caption, tweet, and conversation. When in doubt, return to these principles.

87%
Trust Score
2x
Engagement
✦

Clear

We use plain language and avoid jargon. Our messages are easy to understand on the first read. Complexity is our enemy; clarity is our weapon.

◎

Confident

We state things with conviction. We don't hedge with "we think" or "maybe." We lead with expertise and back it up with evidence.

◇

Human

We write like real people talking to real people. We use contractions, short sentences, and the occasional humor when it fits naturally.

△

Purposeful

Every word earns its place. We don't pad sentences with filler. If a word doesn't add value, it doesn't make the cut.

Tone Spectrum

We Are
  • Direct and honest
  • Warm but professional
  • Informed and authoritative
  • Encouraging and supportive
  • Precise and intentional
We Are Not
  • Corporate or stuffy
  • Aggressive or pushy
  • Condescending or preachy
  • Overly casual or sloppy
  • Vague or evasive
